Jordan Lathon — A role worth evaluating

Jordan Lathon is a 6'5" guard with San Diego Clippers G League experience, available for his next professional opportunity. His March 22, 2025 game against Rip City produced 14 assists, 9 rebounds and 5 steals—a specific professional example of facilitating, rebounding and disrupting possessions in one night. His 2.41 assist-to-turnover ratio across the combined 2024–25 G League sample provides another reason to examine his decision-making. At 6'5", he has the size to compete for a range of guard assignments; individual matchup effectiveness belongs in the film evaluation.

The Piano Mover: Jordan describes his role as bringing energy, communication, physicality and unselfish decisions without needing the offense built around him. Basketball IQ, coachability and commitment to team culture are important parts of the evaluation; former coaches and teammates can speak to those qualities directly.
