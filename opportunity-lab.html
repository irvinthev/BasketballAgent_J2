02 / Current roster — Brooklyn's moving pieces

Long Island opened with 13 players: 10 standard G League players and three Brooklyn two-way forwards — Grant Nelson, Chaney Johnson and Tyler Bilodeau. Nelson and Johnson are the only two who played for Long Island last season. Long Island would still need to choose Jordan for an available standard spot or replace an existing player.
