Evidence and decision standards

What this tool can—and cannot—tell a coach.

Strong: a directly recorded statistic for a specified game. Qualified: a role inference based on that statistic, requiring supporting film. Tentative: a player- or coach-reported trait without independent grading. Positive examples establish capability in selected circumstances, not the rate at which he delivers it. Full-game film, shooting efficiency, errors and opposing matchups are essential counterevidence.
