01 / The player

THE PIANO MOVER.

Jordan's game is built on winning possessions—not chasing touches. He brings high-level point-of-attack defensive intent, size, physical rebounding, unselfish playmaking and the willingness to take the matchup nobody else wants.

02 / Team target

WISCONSIN HERD

Why the call makes sense

Defensive balance

Wisconsin announced a 12-player opening-night roster, with three Milwaukee two-way players. Its backcourt has scoring and creation options, including Kam Jones, Cormac Ryan and other guards.

Jordan could complement that talent: defend the opposing lead handler, generate pressure, keep the offense connected and give existing scorers room to work.

Roster signal

Compact published opening roster. Worth confirming whether there is interest in another defensive-minded guard—not proof of an available slot.

Why Jordan fits

  • 6'5" size for different guard matchups
  • Point-of-attack defense and relentless effort
  • Secondary ball handling and simple reads
  • Rebounding, communication and low-usage value
The GM pitch
“Jordan gives you an experienced G League guard who can take tough defensive assignments, organize possessions and make your scoring guards better without needing plays called for him.”

Performance context: the team's earlier-season scoring and efficiency figures are historical, not proof of its current offense. Wisconsin scored 142 points in its November 6 opener. Lead with the defensive/complementary fit instead.

03 / Team target

LONG ISLAND NETS

Why the call makes sense

Backcourt versatility

Long Island made recent roster decisions that included waiving two guards, Tidjiane Dioumassi and Wooga Poplar. The team still has experienced ball handlers, including Dwight Murray Jr. and Isaiah Wong.

Jordan isn't pitching himself as another high-volume shot creator. He gives a coach a different lineup tool: defense, length, rebounding and connective passing around primary creators.

Roster signal

Recent guard movement creates a useful conversation starter. It does not establish that Long Island plans to sign another guard.

Why Jordan fits

  • Defensive pressure and guard matchup flexibility
  • Facilitation alongside established ball handlers
  • Physical rebounding from the perimeter
  • G League familiarity and team-first mentality
The GM pitch
“If you're looking for another guard who can defend the ball, rebound, move it quickly and compete every possession, Jordan's San Diego film is worth watching.”
04 / Make the introduction

WE HAVE THE PITCH.
NOW MAKE THE CALL.

01

Coach introduction

Ask a former San Diego coach whether they know basketball operations staff at either club—and whether they'd make a quick inquiry.

02

Send targeted film

Share Jordan's full profile, a tight defensive/connector reel, and playmaking film. Lead with the skills each team could use.

03

Turn interest into a tryout

If there's a response, confirm team needs, player rights, availability and the appropriate G League signing route before making commitments.

Copy-ready outreach
“Hey Coach—I've looked at Wisconsin and Long Island as potential fits. Would you be open to asking whether either team is looking for another guard and, if appropriate, putting in a word for me? I've got my profile and film ready.”
